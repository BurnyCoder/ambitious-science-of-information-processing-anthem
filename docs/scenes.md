# Scene guide

Each song section is mapped to a scene id in `content/scenes.toml`, and each id to a component in `video/src/scenes/index.ts`.

**The figures are part of the scenes.** There are 82 figure cards, at least one per sung line. Each is timed to the word it illustrates and carries a provenance badge and a citation ([vignettes.md](vignettes.md), generated). Since v1.5.0 each scene owns its figures:
- **Panels** (lanes a/b): at most two per scene, in slots the scene's layout reserves (`video/src/stage/layout.json`), never over its key objects. A panel stays for its whole time (≥ 3 s), is wired by a connector to the object it explains, and docks there when it ends.
- **Scene-drawn figures** (lane `scene`): ideas the scene can show itself are drawn in place, for example an atlas star's label growing into its formula, or the theory blocks opening to their plots.
- **Nothing dims or covers** the general picture for a figure.

How this works is in [architecture.md](architecture.md) (`src/stage/`), and exp09 measures it ([report](../reports/exp09_unified.md)). v1.4.0's centre "hero shots" are described in [exp08](../reports/exp08_integration.md).

Each scene also draws **concrete examples inside its general picture**. The general picture is never removed: the layer tunnel, the loss surface, the circuit network, the ladder, the point clouds, the pillars, the atlas sphere, the gold halo and the outro token all stay.

**What all scenes have in common:**
- Every scene is a pure function of the frame number.
- Keyword-triggered reveals use `wordTime(tl, section, /regex/)`, the moment the word is sung according to the aligned timings. If the word is missing, they fall back to evenly spaced times.
- Every scene sits on the shared layers: starfield, drifting equation dust (the section's card titles), spectrum ring, the stage, beat/cut flashes, HUD and a citation ticker.
- The scene layer punch-zooms on downbeats and RGB-splits for 0.25 s after each cut.
- Karaoke appears everywhere except the `bigLyrics` scenes (chorus, finale), where the sung word is drawn giant in the lower third instead.
- 2D labels on 3D objects use `projectToScreen` (THREE's own camera projection). Labels that would sit under the hero become margin callouts with a leader line.

## Adding or changing a scene

1. Create `video/src/scenes/MyScene.tsx`. It exports a `React.FC<SceneProps>` and uses `useSceneClock(section)` for timing.
2. Register it in `video/src/scenes/index.ts` with a HUD caption. Set `bigLyrics: true` for giant lyrics in the lower third.
3. Lay it out in `video/src/stage/layout.json` (two panel slots, the protected regions of its key objects, its lyric band; `uv run pytest tests/test_layout.py` checks them), and add a `case` to `video/src/stage/anchors.ts` so its panels are wired to, and dock into, something it draws. Export that geometry from the scene.
4. Map a section to it in `content/scenes.toml`, then re-run `uv run anthem --stage timeline`.
5. Check it with `cd video && node scripts/stills.mjs <frame>`, `--vignettes <section>` or `--docks <section>`, or interactively with `npm run studio`.
