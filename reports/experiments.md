# Experiments: summary

The project is a generative-art pipeline, not a model-training project. Its "experiments" are measurements of the components that decide **when** things happen in the video: which take is used, when each word lights up, and where the beats are. Each one has a question, a criterion fixed before the run, raw data in [`data/`](data), and a dedicated report.

## Glossary

- **Take**: one of the audio versions Suno returns for a single generation request (two per request here).
- **alignedWords / word timings**: Suno's list of sung words, each with start and end in seconds, produced by aligning our lyrics to the generated audio.
- **Coverage**: share of written lyric words exactly matched (after normalisation) to a Suno word flagged `success=true`.
- **hootCer**: Suno's per-take alignment-quality score; its name suggests a character error rate, so lower is better. The exact definition is undocumented.
- **Repair (of timings)**: our rule that moves a mid-line word whose start is implausibly early to just before its own end. v1 (falsified in exp03) used words longer than 1.2 s plus an unconditional gap rule. v2 (shipped) uses only words longer than 3.0 s, and pulls a preceding word along only if its successor was moved.
- **Vocal stem**: the singing voice separated from the instruments by a source-separation model (here Demucs). It still contains the choir and backing vocals.
- **Forced alignment**: finding *when* each word of a *known* text is spoken or sung. A CTC model gives per-frame letter probabilities, and a Viterbi search places the transcript's letters in order through them. Unlike a recogniser such as Whisper, it cannot mishear words.
- **CTC (connectionist temporal classification)**: a training objective for sequence models whose outputs are per-frame symbol probabilities plus a "blank". Its posteriors are "peaky", which can shift boundaries by a few frames.
- **Replace section (exp06)**: a Suno API operation that re-sings a time window (≥ 10 s) of an existing take with new lyrics and blends it into the rest.
- **Anchor (exp05)**: a hand-labelled acoustic onset of a line's first word, read blind from a plot of the vocal stem. It is used to check the absolute timing of the forced aligner.
- **Signed offset δ**: the median of (Suno onset − reference onset). Positive means Suno is late. A global word-time offset of −δ undoes it.
- **Line-cluster bootstrap**: a bootstrap that resamples whole lyric lines instead of single words, because words in one line share timing errors. It gives honest, wider confidence intervals.
- **Onset**: the time a word or sound begins.
- **Figure card / illustration track (exp07)**: a small panel timed to the sung word it illustrates, with a provenance badge and a citation. The 86 cards are listed in [docs/vignettes.md](../docs/vignettes.md).
- **Provenance badge**: says where a card's content comes from: released data (files from the authors or a well-known third party), published numbers (quoted from a paper), live math (computed in the video), from the paper (a formula or claim), or schematic (our illustration).
- **Attribution graph / supernode**: a graph of which internal features of a model cause which others and the output, for one prompt (Lindsey et al. 2025). Supernodes are the authors' hand-made groups of related features, such as "say Austin".
- **Grokking**: a model fits its training data early and only much later generalizes to held-out data (Power et al. 2022). In Nanda et al.'s modular-addition model, the general solution uses a few Fourier "key frequencies".
- **Visual density proxy**: the size of a frame after lossless PNG compression; more detail compresses less well.
- **Hero shot / dock (exp08)**: since v1.4.0 a figure card does not sit in a corner box.
  - It dives out of the scene object its lyric names: the *anchor*, such as a ladder rung or an atlas star.
  - It is shown large at centre stage, the *hero*, for at most 3.2 s.
  - It then recedes into depth and, at its end, flies back and *docks* into the object as a thumbnail.
- **Heroness**: a number from 0 to 1 saying how much a card is the hero right now. The card counts as the hero when it is ≥ 0.5.
- **Panel slot / protected region (exp09)**: since v1.5.0 each scene's layout (`video/src/stage/layout.json`) reserves at most two rectangles for figure panels, and lists the rectangles of its key objects (the ladder, the SGD swarm, the closing statement...) that no panel may cover.
- **Scene-drawn figure (lane `scene`)**: a figure the scene draws itself, in place. For example, an atlas star's label grows into its formula.
- **Rewind**: the outro's montage of earlier moments of the video, each played backwards on beat-timed cuts that get faster, drawn with Remotion's `<Freeze>`.
- **Overview mosaic (exp10)**: the finale's grid of ten live tiles, one per earlier section, each a `<Freeze>` copy of the video at that section's moment with the most figure cards, playing on at half speed.
- **Vocal envelope (exp10)**: the loudness of the separated singing voice over time (RMS per video frame of the Demucs vocal stem, smoothed over 0.4 s); since v1.6.0 it sets the size of the bridge's atlas sphere.
- **Bright-pixel centroid (exp10)**: the average x position of the pixels brighter than 160 (of 255) in a band of the frame; used as a simple measure of whether text or an object is horizontally centred.
- **Non-inferiority test**: asks "is the new version not worse by more than a margin fixed in advance?" rather than "is it better?". It passes when the whole confidence interval lies above −margin.
- **Beat / downbeat**: the regular pulse of the music, and the first beat of each bar (assumed 4 beats per bar here).
- **Hop size**: the number of audio samples between successive analysis frames. Smaller hops give finer time resolution (256 samples at 22.05 kHz is 11.6 ms).
- **Tempogram tempo vs median beat spacing**: two tempo estimates. The first is quantized to whole frame lags. The second is 60 / median(time between tracked beats).
- **F-measure (±70 ms)**: harmonic mean of precision (detected beats that are real) and recall (real beats that were detected), counting a hit when a detected beat lies within 70 ms of a true beat.
- **95% CI**: confidence interval, a range that would contain the true mean in 95% of repeated experiments (t-interval) or, for medians, a bootstrap percentile range.
- **Wilcoxon signed-rank test**: a non-parametric paired test of whether one condition's errors are systematically smaller than another's.
- **Remotion composition / Sequence**: a video defined as React components. A `<Sequence>` places a component at a frame range.
- **Determinism**: rendering frame *n* always gives the same image, which is required because frames render in parallel browser tabs.
